Our story

Why Maqsusi EduTech exists

Maqsusi EduTech was founded on a simple idea: training should end in outcomes, not just certificates. As a recognized Telangana Skill Development Partner, we design curriculum around what hiring partners actually look for — practical skill, portfolio-ready projects, and interview readiness.

Today our programs span software development, enterprise systems, business and data tools, communication and test prep, and dedicated tracks for young learners — serving everyone from Nursery-age kids to working professionals looking to upskill.
